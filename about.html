Who We Are

HEDS International (PVT) LTD.

HEDS International (PVT) LTD. is an educational institution, established in 2009, located in Lahore, Pakistan. HEDS International is a leading foreign education consultancy firm in Pakistan dedicated to assisting local and international students in getting admission placement in well-reputed educational institutes worldwide. This institution is managed and run by a group of experienced persons with an objective to provide a wide range of information and advice to our prospective students seeking admission to different colleges and universities abroad.

We provide general information about study abroad. We hold information about colleges and universities in various countries, financial aid available to support students in their education, the student visa process, living costs, student life and much more.

Mission Statement

Our Mission is to assist those students who are really looking for an overseas career as well as permanent settlement, to realize their dreams of migrating to a country that offers one of the best educational and living standards in the world. The company has distinct quality standards for practice which in turn ensures that clients are properly and consistently represented and advised. The company acknowledges ethical and professional responsibility to clients and candidates. Company management and its working team are fully devoted and struggling hard to achieve its mission and maintain it too.
