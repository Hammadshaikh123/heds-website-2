Sweden Study Visa Pakistan: Complete Guide

HEDS International · Visa

Getting your Sweden study visa (residence permit) sorted is the last major milestone between admission and actually starting your program. This guide walks through the complete journey, step by step, specifically for Pakistani students.

Step 1: Secure Your Unconditional Admission

Your visa application cannot proceed on a conditional offer. Make sure all conditions — final transcripts, English proficiency proof, degree certificates — are cleared with the university first.

Step 2: Pay Your Tuition Fee

Once your offer is unconditional, pay the required tuition installment as instructed by the university. Keep the official payment receipt — this is a mandatory document for your visa file.

Step 3: Arrange Proof of Funds

This is the step where most applications get delayed or rejected. You'll need to show:

  • Funds available in your own name (or a combination of your own funds and a scholarship/loan, depending on the rules that year)
  • A consistent bank statement history — a single large lump-sum deposit right before applying can raise questions
  • Funds that meet or exceed the minimum monthly requirement published by the Swedish Migration Agency for the full duration of your program

Step 4: Get Health Insurance

Depending on your program length and nationality-specific rules, you may need to arrange health insurance coverage before or immediately after arrival.

Step 5: Submit Your Online Application

Applications go through the Swedish Migration Agency's official online portal. You'll fill in your personal details, upload your documents, and pay the application fee.

Step 6: Visit the Visa Application Centre

After the online step, Pakistani applicants typically need to visit a visa application centre to submit biometric data (fingerprints and photo).

Step 7: Wait for a Decision

Processing times vary depending on the season and how complete your file is. Applying as early as possible after receiving your unconditional offer significantly reduces the risk of a delayed decision.

Step 8: Prepare for Arrival

Once approved, you'll receive your residence permit card information. From there, plan your travel, accommodation, and registration with your university's international office.

Documents Checklist (Typical)

  • Valid passport
  • Unconditional admission letter
  • Tuition payment proof
  • Proof of funds
  • Health insurance (if required)
  • Passport photographs
  • Any program-specific documents requested by the Migration Agency

Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying before your admission is fully unconditional
  • Submitting unclear or inconsistent bank statements
  • Waiting too long after admission to start the visa process
  • Assuming last year's rules still apply without checking for updates
