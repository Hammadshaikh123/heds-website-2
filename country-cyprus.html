Study in Cyprus for Pakistani Students

Lower cost of living, English-taught programs and an easier entry point into Europe.

Cyprus offers internationally recognized education in a safe, welcoming, and multicultural environment at an affordable cost. Its universities provide modern, career-focused programs with strong emphasis on business, hospitality, healthcare, and technology. Situated at the crossroads of Europe, Asia, and Africa, Cyprus gives students a unique blend of European education, Mediterranean lifestyle, and valuable international exposure.

Why Study in Cyprus?

Cyprus offers world-class education with a focus on practical learning and career readiness. With English-taught programs, affordable tuition fees, and a safe, welcoming environment, Cyprus is an excellent choice for Pakistani students. The country's strategic location at the crossroads of Europe, Asia, and Africa provides students with unique cultural exposure and international career opportunities.

Admission Requirements

Bachelor's

HSSC with passing grades; IELTS 5.5–6.5 as per university requirement

Master's

16 years of education with passing grades; IELTS 6.0–7.0 as per university requirement

PhD

Master's degree with research experience; IELTS 6.5+

Visa

Student visa – financial proof, health insurance, accommodation proof

Scholarships for Pakistani Students

Merit-Based University Awards

  • Tuition fee waivers for strong academic profiles
  • Varying by university
  • 10-50% fee reduction

Early Applicant Discounts

  • Reduced fees for early enrollment
  • Available at several universities
  • Up to 20% fee waiver

Need-Based Scholarships

  • Available for students with financial need
  • Partial tuition fee waiver
  • Apply through university

Sports & Talent Scholarships

  • For exceptional athletes and artists
  • Varies by university
