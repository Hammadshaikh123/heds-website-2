Study in China

Study in China for Pakistani Students

Affordable tuition, strong engineering and medical programs, wide university choice.

China has become a leading destination for international students, offering high-quality education at an affordable cost. Its universities are renowned for excellence in engineering, medicine, business, and technology, supported by world-class research facilities and modern campuses. Studying in China also provides students with the opportunity to experience one of the world's fastest-growing economies while gaining valuable global and cultural exposure.

Why Study in China?

China offers world-class education with a focus on innovation and practical learning. With globally recognised universities, affordable tuition fees, and strong scholarship opportunities, China is a top choice for Pakistani students. The country's rich cultural heritage, modern infrastructure, and growing economy make it an ideal destination for international students.

Admission Requirements

Bachelor's

HSSC with passing grades; no IELTS/English proficiency test required

Master's

16 years of education with passing grades; no IELTS/English proficiency test required

PhD

Master's degree with research experience; no IELTS required

Visa

Student visa (X1 for >180 days, X2 for <180 days) – JW202 form required

Scholarships for Pakistani Students

Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)

  • Fully funded by Chinese Government
  • Free tuition + free hostel + living allowance
  • Open to Pakistani students

Confucius Institute Scholarship

  • Free tuition + free hostel + living allowance
  • For Chinese language and culture programs
  • Merit-based

University-Specific Awards

  • Fully funded options available
  • Partially funded: 50% to 70% fee waiver
  • Available at most universities

Pakistani Government Scholarships

  • HEC scholarships for China
  • Under various bilateral agreements
  • Check HEC website for details
Figures and requirements above are general guidance and change frequently — confirm current tuition, fees and intake dates with your HEDS counsellor before applying.
