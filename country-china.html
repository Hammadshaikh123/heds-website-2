Study in China for Pakistani Students 2026

Affordable tuition, strong engineering and medical programs, wide university choice.

Average Tuition Fee
Generally lower than Western destinations; varies by university
Average Living Cost
RMB 1,000 to 1,500 per month

Admission Requirements

Bachelor's

HSSC with passing grades; no IELTS/English proficiency test required

Master's

16 years of education with passing grades; no IELTS/English proficiency test required

Scholarship Options

Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC)

  • Free tuition
  • Free hostel
  • Living allowance

Confucius Institute Scholarship

  • Free tuition
  • Free hostel
  • Living allowance

University-Specific Awards for Pakistani Students

  • Fully funded options
  • Partially funded: 50% to 70% fee waiver
