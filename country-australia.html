Study in Australia for Pakistani Students

Globally recognised degrees, multicultural cities and strong post-study work rights.

Australia is one of the world's most popular study destinations, renowned for its globally ranked universities, cutting-edge research, and industry-focused education. Students benefit from practical learning, strong graduate employability, and excellent opportunities to gain real-world experience while studying. With its multicultural society, vibrant cities, and outstanding quality of life, Australia offers an unforgettable educational journey and a strong foundation for global career success.

Why Study in Australia?

Australia offers world-class education with a focus on innovation and practical learning. With globally recognised universities, diverse course options, and excellent post-study work opportunities, Australia is a top choice for Pakistani students. The country's multicultural environment, high standard of living, and strong economy make it an ideal destination for international students.

Admission Requirements

Bachelor's

HSSC with passing grades; IELTS 6.0–7.0 as per university requirement

Master's

16 years of education with passing grades; IELTS 6.5–7.5 as per university requirement

PhD

Master's degree with research experience; IELTS 7.0+

Visa

Student visa (subclass 500) – Genuine Student requirement, financial proof, health insurance

Scholarships for Pakistani Students

Australia Awards Scholarship

  • Fully funded by Australian Government
  • Tuition + living expenses + travel
  • Open to Pakistani students

Destination Australia

  • Up to AUD 15,000 per year
  • For studying in regional areas
  • Merit-based

University Scholarships

  • 10-50% tuition fee waiver
  • Merit-based and need-based
  • Available at most universities

Research Scholarships

  • For Master's and PhD students
  • Full tuition + stipend
  • RTP (Research Training Program)
Figures and requirements above are general guidance and change frequently — confirm current tuition, fees and intake dates with your HEDS counsellor before applying.
